# SIGNAL '85 — Story Bible

Source of truth for lore. All names here are original; keep it that way.

## Setting
**Hollow Creek, autumn 1985.** A quiet small town: one main street, glowing shop windows, sodium street lamps, and an old red **radio tower** at the north end of the road that has only ever played late-night music and weather reports.

## The inciting event
One October night the tower picks up a signal nobody sent — a pulse like a heartbeat made of static. That same night a red ring of light tears open in the field east of Main Street: **the rift**.

## The Undergrid
The other side of the rift. An exact copy of Hollow Creek, but dead: red street lights, black **tendrils** over the buildings, pale **spores** hanging in cold blue air. Something there has been listening to our world for a long time.

## The Hollowers
Tall shadow creatures. Their heads open like dark flowers around a glowing red maw. They drift **through** walls rather than around them and hunt anything that crosses over.

## The Shiftframes and their pilots (the players)
Years ago a government lab outside town — **Project LOWBAND** — built machines to explore "other frequencies." When the program was shut down, the machines were hidden and forgotten. When the rift opened, they woke up — and each one **chose a pilot** from the kids of Hollow Creek. Pilot and machine are synced: the armor folds around the pilot when they suit up. Shiftframes are armored robots that fold into fast cars.
- **Pilot:** the teen themselves, in their own 80s style; walks around town, can't fight.
- **Robot mode:** suited up, armored, can fire the arm blaster.
- **Vehicle mode:** very fast, no weapon.

## The mission
The strange signal shattered across the Undergrid as glowing blue **Signal Shards**. Fed into the **Signal Console** under the radio tower, they let the tower broadcast a counter-signal that seals the rift.

Round loop: cross the rift → find shards → fight (robot) or flee (vehicle) → race shards home → seal the rift.

## Why it never ends
Each time the rift is sealed it reopens. Something deeper in the Undergrid is still transmitting.

## Chapters
1. **Seal the Rift** — collect Signal Shards in the Undergrid and feed them to the Signal Console. The rift closes... but the signal does not stop.
2. **The Source** — sealing the rift only cut one line. The signal is now fed by **Hive Nodes**, pulsing growths rooted in the Undergrid. Destroying all three wakes their guardian, **the Mind Warden**, a giant Hollower that has been listening from beneath the Undergrid's radio tower. Defeat it and the signal finally goes quiet... for now.
3. **LOWBAND** — the signal returns, weaker, and the town kids trace it to the abandoned **Project LOWBAND lab** in the woods west of town (empty since 1979). Five **LOWBAND files** (three in the lab, two in its Undergrid copy) reveal that the scientists built **the Carrier**, a machine to "listen to other frequencies" — and something listened back. The Shiftframes were built to "go where people can't". Restarting three **power relays** in the Undergrid lets the Carrier shut down safely; the Shiftframes defend it while it powers down, then race its shutdown code to the radio tower, and WHCK 85.5 FM broadcasts it. The signal stops... until a single pulse comes from far beneath the Undergrid. A last file: *"LOWBAND was not the first. Site 2."*
4. **Site 2** — Dot works out that Site 2 was never in the woods: it is **under the Grand Hotel**, which was built in 1958 as its cover. Behind a cracked tile wall in the floor 1 men's washroom is a steel door with four card slots. Four **keycards** (two in the town hotel, two in its Undergrid copy) open it. Below lie LOWBAND's first tunnels: 1950s concrete, reel-to-reel computers, flooded corridors. Four **Echo Pylons** are still recording the signal and must be destroyed. They are guarded by **Echoes**, shadow copies of Shiftframes that shoot back. In the deepest room a 1962 tape of the head scientist, **Dr. Helen Marsh**, plays: *"We didn't open the door. We only knocked. Something on the other side has been answering ever since. We call it the Listener."* Every radio in town switches on by itself.
5. **Static Storm** — the Listener knows. The Undergrid starts **leaking into Hollow Creek itself**: red static clouds, tendrils on the shops, small rifts tearing open on Main Street. The pilots close five **mini-rifts** in town, defend the **WHCK 85.5 van** while its DJ broadcasts a calming signal, then race the van's signal tapes from the radio tower to the hotel as the road cracks. The storm calms... and a huge rift opens under Ricky. **Ricky is pulled into the Undergrid.** His last words over the radio: *"...guys? I can see it. It's so big..."*
6. **The Listener** (Season 1 finale) — beneath Site 2 lies **the Deep Grid**, a dark world of broken radio towers, floating pieces of Hollow Creek and humming cable. The pilots descend and break the three **static locks** on Ricky's cage. Then they face **the Listener**, a colossal shape of static and antennas with a great glowing "ear":
   - **Phase 1:** shoot off its 4 antennas.
   - **Phase 2:** a car chase over the collapsing road.
   - **Phase 3:** all pilots hold the three tower rings while WHCK and Dot broadcast the counter-signal.

   The Listener falls silent, the rift closes and **stays closed**, and Hollow Creek wakes to a normal morning. The Shiftframes power down in the garage — and in the very last shot one of them flickers back on (Season 2 hook).

## Characters
- **Dot** and **Ricky** — Hollow Creek kids who talk to the Shiftframes over walkie-talkies (channel 4). Dot is calm and reads the files; Ricky is excitable and watches the town.
- **Dr. Helen Marsh** — head scientist of Project LOWBAND, heard only on old tapes (1958–1962). She named the Listener.
- **The WHCK DJ** — the late-night voice of WHCK 85.5 FM; broadcasts the calming signal in Chapter 5 and the counter-signal in Chapter 6.

## Creatures
- **Hollowers** — see above.
- **Static Hounds** — low, fast, crackling shapes that hunt in packs of three. Faster than a robot on foot, weak to the blaster; only a Shiftframe in car mode can outrun them. First seen in Chapter 3.

- **Echoes** — dark shadow copies of Shiftframes made by the Echo Pylons. They keep their distance and shoot back. First seen in Chapter 4.
- **The Listener** — the thing on the other side of LOWBAND's door, the source of the signal. Final boss of Season 1 (Chapter 6).

## Places
- **Site 2** — LOWBAND's first tunnels, under the Grand Hotel (Chapter 4).
- **The Deep Grid** — the world beneath the Undergrid where the Listener lives (Chapter 6).

## Future story hooks
- **Season 2** — one Shiftframe powers back on after the finale.
- **WHCK 85.5 FM** — the radio tower's station; used for broadcasts, intros and the finales.
