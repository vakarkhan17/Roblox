## Story script

**Opening (banner + walkie-talkie lines in the HUD)**
> *Static.* "...is anyone there? This is Dot, on channel 4. The tower picked up the signal again — weaker, but it's back."
> "Ricky here. My dad says the signal is coming from the old LOWBAND lab out in the woods. Nobody's been in there since '79."
> "Shiftframes... that's where you came from, isn't it? Be careful. Something in there is still switched on."

**Stage 1 — The Files** (`Stage = "Files"`, Goal 5)
The lab is dark and half-flooded with Undergrid growth. Five LOWBAND file folders tell what happened: the scientists built a machine, **the Carrier**, to "listen to other frequencies" — and something listened back. Three files are in the real lab, two only in its Undergrid copy, so players must cross the rift back and forth.
> Dot: "File 3 says they *built you to go where people can't*. Wow."

**Stage 2 — The Relays** (`Stage = "Relays"`, Goal 3)
The Carrier needs power to be shut down safely. Three relay circles around the lab: stand inside a glowing circle for 15 s to restart it (progress pauses when you leave). Hollowers and new **Static Hounds** swarm the relays.
> Ricky: "Two more! The lights in town are flickering — hurry!"

**Stage 3 — The Carrier** (`Stage = "Carrier"`)
The Carrier wakes and fights back. Defend it for 2 minutes while it powers down (waves of Hollowers and Hounds; the Carrier has a health bar — if it breaks, the stage restarts). When it's done, it prints a shutdown code: race in **car mode** to the radio tower console within **60 s** to broadcast it on WHCK 85.5 FM.
> Dot, live on the radio: "This is WHCK 85.5, Hollow Creek... if you can hear this — it's working!"

**Ending (cliffhanger)**
The signal stops... then a single pulse comes from *far beneath the Undergrid*. A last file, unlocked after the credits banner: *"LOWBAND was not the first. Site 2."* → `TO BE CONTINUED IN CHAPTER 4`.
